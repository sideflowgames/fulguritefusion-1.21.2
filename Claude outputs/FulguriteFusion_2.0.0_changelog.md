# Fulgurite Fusion 2.0.0: The Stormreach

The storm has a home. 2.0 opens the Stormreach, an end-game dimension above the clouds, along with the road to reach it: three Storm Guardians, the Storm Titan, and the rift that only the worthy can cross.

## New: The road to the Stormreach

- **Storm Guardians:** three summoned bosses, one in each dimension.
    - **Thunderhead** (Overworld): use a Charged Spark Cell on a Lightning Rod under open sky during a thunderstorm.
    - **Magma Conduit** (Nether): throw a Charged Spark Cell into lava.
    - **Void Arc** (The End): use an End Crystal on a Lightning Rod.
    - Each always drops its key piece: the Storm Eye, the Molten Heart or the Void Shard.
- **Storm Sigil:** forge the three key pieces with a Charged Spark Cell in the Storm Forge. Use it to travel to the Titan Trials, bringing anyone within 8 blocks with you.
- **The Storm Titan:** wake it at the Titan Altar in your own arena. Bring friends; it grows stronger for every challenger. Read the altar before you fight...
- **Titan armor:** the Titan drops one random piece each time it falls. Offer a Stormforged-or-better piece on the altar first to aim for that piece. A full set keeps every Prime ability and adds storm regeneration, a lightning aura, no knockback, fire and lava immunity, and a static dash.
- **The Stormreach Rift:** beating the Titan gives you a Rift Core. Build a Rift Frame with a Rift Keystone and a Lightning Rod, and let lightning open it. Only players who have defeated the Titan can pass through.

## New: The Stormreach

- A vast continent of Stormstone above the clouds, split by ravines, with deep caves and caverns down to bedrock.
- **Its own storms:** long storms of 10 to 20 minutes with short calm spells. Lightning strikes all around you, and fuses Charged Stormsand into Fulgurite Glass.
- **Twenty lands:** from Stormgrass Plains, Conductor Forests and Ion Lakes to Voltcap Mushroom Fields and Rubberwood Jungle, plus rarer lands like the Aurora Tundra, Tempest Peaks, Petrified Forest, Magnetite Fields, Crystal Caverns Rim, Fulgurite Badlands, Ashen Scar and Stormglass Desert.
- **New blocks and plants:** Charged Loam and Arcgrass, Cloudmoss, three new trees (Conductor, Thunderoak, Sparkbush), glowing flowers for dyes, Ion Bulbs, Voltcaps that grow into giant mushrooms, and Stormstone in polished and brick forms.
- **New ores:** Magnetite, Argentite (silver), Aurorite, Cinderite, Prismite, Stormquartz, Fossil Amber and Tempest Crystal. Magnetite and Argentite also generate deep in the Overworld. Raw Magnetite and Raw Argentite double in the Crusher.
- **Creatures:** Sparkhorns, tameable Pufflings, Rubberback Sloths (bucket them for latex), Voltcap Snails, Glasscrabs, Static Jellies, Ion Eels and Glimmerwings. Below ground: Arcwurms, Crystal Stalkers, Static Wraiths and Shockroach swarms. In storms: Stormhound packs and Voltcap Crawlers.

## New: Stormreach hostiles

- Beasts far tougher than the Overworld's. Their storm damage goes through normal armor, and their static field drains you unless you wear a full Titan set.
- More of them come out in a storm, and a beast born in a storm is Storm-charged: more health and harder hits.
- **Roamers:** Arc Reaver, Thunderhulk, Stormcaller Shade, Static Leech, Ion Swarm, Voltspitter, Tempest Raptor and Gloomarc Spider.
- **Signature beasts:** four rare elites with boss bars, one per land: Peak Colossus, Cinder Behemoth, Aurora Wraith and Prism Sentinel.
- Every beast drops its own part.

## New: Stormreach dungeons

- Four great dungeons, each with more than thirty rooms: **Stormspire**, **Ashen Foundry**, **Aurora Sanctum** and **Prism Vault**.
- Follow the light to the guardian. Storm Cages, Arc Traps and Tesla Coils guard the halls, and each dungeon has its own danger.
- A guardian waits at the end, and a sealed treasure vault beside it. Each dungeon can be cleared only once.
- Each holds a material found nowhere else: Skyforged Plate, Foundry Heartstone, Aurora Essence and Prism Core.
- **Relics:** rare third-tier trinkets: Skyrender Charm, Foundry Heart, Aurora Loop and Prism Eye.

## New: Titan Prime

- **Titan Forge:** a new multiblock that fuses a Prime armor piece with its matching Titan piece and Stormreach ores into **Titan Prime armor**, the strongest armor in the mod.

## New: Trinkets and the equipment panel

- Press **Gear** in your inventory for the equipment panel: a slot for each trinket and a Back slot for a backpack, so you can wear one with a chestplate. With Curios installed, trinkets use Curios slots instead.
- **Guardian trinkets** (1 in 4 drop): Cloudstep Charm, Conduit Heart and Void Arc Band.
- **Crafted trinkets:** Grounding Charm, Cinder Ward and Seer's Ring.
- **Empowered trinkets:** forge any trinket in the Storm Forge for a stronger version.

## New: Storm brews and gadgets

- **Nine brews** made from Stormreach parts in a brewing stand, including Ion Tonic, Volt Draught (puts 250,000 Fm into your gear), Hulk's Brew, Veil Draught, Leech Tonic, Skystep Draught, Ember Elixir, Rime Draught and Prism Sight.
- **Gadgets:** Ion Lamp, Storm Ward (stops storm beasts spawning within 32 blocks while powered), Updraft Vent, Silk Rope and Reaver Spikes.

## New: Shock Farm

- A Fm-powered mob farm: a 5x5 or 7x7 multiblock of Galvanic Soil, Tesla Prongs and a controller, walled with glass.
- Spawns the mobs of the biome it stands in (no bosses or rare mobs) and electrocutes them. Drops and XP go into a Farm Collector; power goes in through a Farm Power Port.
- The whole farm merges into one animated model, with a ghost build to help you place it.
- **Controller menu:** power, status, an ON/OFF switch and the biome's spawn list.
- **Upgrade cards:** 2 slots on a 5x5 farm, 4 on a 7x7.
    - **Mob Filter Card:** right-click a mob to set it, and the farm spawns only that mob.
    - **Speed Card I-III:** faster spawns, at a higher Fm cost.
    - **Efficiency Card I-III:** less Fm per spawn and zap.
    - **Looting Card I-III:** Looting on kills, plus more XP.

## New: Item transport

- **Item Pipes:** Copper, Fulgurite and Stormforged, moving 2, 16 and 32 items a tick over any distance. Click a side to set Insert, Extract or Off, and watch the items slide past the glass.
- **Pipe filters:** Fulgurite and Stormforged pipes filter up to 9 items, as an allow list or a block list.
- **Arc Transfer:** link an Arc Sender and an Arc Receiver up to 5 blocks apart with the Arc Tuner. Once a second, the Sender throws up to 27 stacks across in a single bolt, for 400 Fm.

## Changed

- The Storm Titan hits less hard.
- The guide book has new chapters for everything above.
